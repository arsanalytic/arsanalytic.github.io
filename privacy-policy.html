Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 November 2026. Last updated: 14 November 2026.

This policy describes how the Classic Analytics Android app handles your information. In short: the app shows you your own Google Analytics and Search Console data, it stores your sign-in on your own phone, and it sends your data nowhere else.

Information the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app requests read-only access to:

  • Your Google account — your name, email address, and profile photo, so the app knows who is signed in. This uses the standard Google Sign-In scopes (openid, email, profile).
  • Your Google Analytics data — sessions, users, pages, sources, countries, devices, and conversions for the Analytics properties you choose. This uses the Analytics read-only scope.
  • Your Google Search Console data — search queries, clicks, impressions, and positions for your sites. This uses the Search Console read-only scope.

The app only reads data from accounts and properties you sign in to and select yourself. It cannot see anyone else's data.

How your information is used

Your data is used for one purpose only: to display your own analytics to you inside the app. Nothing else is done with it.

Where your information is stored

Your sign-in tokens are stored securely on your own device. Analytics results are cached on your own device so screens open fast. There is no server run by us for this app: the app talks directly to Google's own APIs (Google Sign-In, Google Analytics, and Google Search Console) and to nowhere else.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.
  • We do not share your data with any third party other than Google's own services listed above, which are needed for the app to work.
  • We do not collect your data for our own analytics, tracking, or profiling.

Third-party services

The app uses Google Sign-In, Google Analytics, and Google Search Console. Your use of those Google services is covered by Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices and deletion

Signing out of the app removes your stored sign-in tokens from your device. Uninstalling the app removes the cached analytics data from your device. If you want your Google account access revoked, you can also remove Classic Analytics from your Google account's third-party access settings at any time.

Children

Classic Analytics is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new last-updated date.
