What it is

Google Analytics 4 replaced Universal Analytics, and with it went the simple reports many site owners relied on: one Dashboard with your key numbers, Realtime, Audience, Acquisition, Behaviour — each report one tap away, every row tappable to drill deeper. Classic Analytics brings that layout back, reading your data straight from Google's Analytics and Search Console APIs.

Who it is for: bloggers, small site owners, and anyone who checks their traffic every day and misses the old layout. If you run a site on Google Analytics, this is your numbers, the way you remember them.

What you can see

  • Dashboard — your key numbers at a glance, with percentage changes against the previous period.
  • Realtime — who is on your site right now, and which pages they are reading.
  • Audience — new vs returning visitors, devices, languages, and countries.
  • Acquisition — channels, sources, and exact referrers, with drill-downs to their pages.
  • Behaviour — all pages and landing pages, and any page's traffic by source.
  • Conversions — your conversion events and their trend.
  • AI traffic — sessions arriving from AI assistants, and the pages they land on.
  • Search keywords — the queries bringing people to your site, from Google Search Console, including the ones almost on page one.
  • Traffic by time — sessions by hour and by day of the week, so you can see when your readers are online.
  • Morning summary (Android only) — yesterday's sessions, top page, and biggest mover, once a day.

How it works

Sign in with your Google account and pick one of your Analytics properties. The app reads your data through Google's own Analytics and Search Console APIs, with read-only access. Your sign-in stays on your own device, your data stays on your own device, and there is no server in between. We never sell your data and never use it for advertising. Read the full Privacy Policy.

Web app or Android app?

Web app — free, runs in any browser at arsanalytic.github.io/app/, nothing to install. It works on any device with a browser, including iPhone. Your sign-in is stored in your browser on your own device. There are no notifications on the web version; the morning summary is Android-only.

Android app — the same reports as a native app, plus the morning summary notification. It is in closed testing while the Play Store listing is prepared; for now you can install it directly from the link above.

Frequently asked questions

What data does the app read?

Your Google Analytics 4 data — sessions, users, pages, sources, countries, devices, and conversions for the properties you choose — and your Google Search Console data — search queries, clicks, impressions, and positions for your sites.

Can it change anything in my Analytics account?

No. The app asks Google for read-only access only. It can display your data, and nothing else.

Where does my data go?

Nowhere. Your sign-in tokens are stored on your own device — in secure storage on Android, in your browser's storage on the web version — and the app talks directly to Google's own APIs. There is no server run by us, and we never see your data.

Which properties work?

Any Google Analytics 4 property your Google account has access to. Sign in, pick a property from the list, or paste a property ID if you know it. (Universal Analytics properties stopped collecting data in 2023, so this app works with GA4 properties.)

Why isn't it called Universal Analytics?

Universal Analytics is Google's product name. Classic Analytics is a new, independent app that shows your GA4 data in that classic layout — it is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google.

Does it work on iPhone?

There is no iPhone app yet, but the web app runs in any browser, including on iPhone — open arsanalytic.github.io/app/ and sign in.

Status: The web app is live and free. The Android app is in closed testing while the Play Store listing is prepared.
