Classic Analytics

Your Google Analytics data, the classic way.

Classic Analytics is an Android app that shows your Google Analytics 4 data in the familiar old Universal Analytics layout: the reports you already know, fed by Google's own data. No new system to learn, no numbers from anywhere else.

What you can see

  • Dashboard — your key numbers at a glance, with percentage changes against the previous period.
  • Realtime — who is on your site right now, and which pages they are reading.
  • Audience — new vs returning visitors, devices, and countries.
  • Acquisition — sources, channels, and exact referrers, with drill-downs to their pages.
  • Behaviour — all pages and landing pages, and any page's traffic by source.
  • Conversions — your conversion events and their trend.
  • AI traffic — sessions arriving from AI assistants, and the pages they land on.
  • Search keywords — the queries bringing people to your site, from Google Search Console, including the ones almost on page one.
  • Traffic by time — sessions by hour and by day of the week, so you can see when your readers are online.
  • Morning summary — yesterday's sessions, top page, and biggest mover, once a day.

How it works

Sign in with your Google account and pick one of your Analytics properties. The app reads your data through Google's own Analytics and Search Console APIs, with read-only access. Your sign-in stays on your own phone, your data stays on your own phone, and there is no server in between. We never sell your data and never use it for advertising. Read the full Privacy Policy.
